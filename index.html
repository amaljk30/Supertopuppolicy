Pre-Acceptance Medical Check-Up Rules

Required at Proposer's Cost (50% reimbursed on acceptance) for:

  • Fresh entrants with adverse medical history
  • Fresh entrants aged above 60 years (entry allowed up to 65 yrs)
  • Persons aged above 60 years with a break in insurance
  • Members requesting Sum Insured enhancement at renewal

Reports valid for 30 days: Physical Exam (MD/MS), CBC, Urine R/M, HbA1c, Lipid Profile, Serum Creatinine, SGOT/SGPT, ECG.
